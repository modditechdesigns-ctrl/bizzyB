Our story

Made busy. Made better.

Bizzy 'B' Bites started with a simple idea: fast food doesn't have to mean boring food. We're a small kitchen on Nsukka Street in Garki, serving the meals we grew up eating — jollof, suya, puff-puff, grilled chicken — made fresh every day.

Whether you're grabbing lunch between meetings, feeding the family, or craving a midnight snack, we make it taste like home. From our kitchen to your table, every bite celebrates the bold, comforting flavours of Nigerian street food.
